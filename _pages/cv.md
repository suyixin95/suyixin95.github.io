Research Experience (研究经历)
======
* **Coupling Mechanism and Design Methods of Magnetic Bearings and Support Components (Core Member, 2019.01–now)**
  * **磁悬浮轴承与支承部件耦合作用机理及设计方法研究（核心骨干，2019.01–至今）**
  * National Key R&D Program — High-speed Precision Suspension Bearings; principal author of the project proposal and a core member.
  * 国家重点研发计划——高速精密悬浮轴承，立项申请书主要撰写者及核心骨干。
  * Studied the nonlinear dynamic characteristics and behavior identification of magnetic bearing–rotor–touchdown bearing systems, and proposed an integrated control method combining nonlinear passive isolation, feedforward control, and re-levitation control, which improves environmental adaptability under base or rotor shock loads and solves the challenge of self-recovering to a stable non-contact state after rub-impact instability.
  * 开展磁轴承-转子-保护轴承系统非线性动力学特性及行为辨识研究，创新提出非线性被动隔振、前馈控制与重悬浮控制算法相结合的综合控制方法，提高系统对基础或转子冲击载荷的环境适应性，解决了转子碰摩失稳时能自恢复为非接触稳定状态的技术难题。

* **Vibration Isolation Applications of Magnetic Suspension Technology (Core Member, 2019.06–now)**

  * 装备预研领域基金，立项申请书主要撰写者及核心骨干。
  * Built a coupled dynamic model of a flexible rotor, magnetic bearings, and elastic base support; revealed the vibration transmission mechanism between active magnetic-bearing control and passive base elasticity; and proposed a structural optimization matching design together with an adaptive feedforward active isolation strategy.
  * 针对磁悬浮高速旋转机械减振需求，建立柔性转子-磁悬浮轴承-基础弹性支承耦合动力学模型，获取磁悬浮轴承主动控制与基础被动支承弹性耦合的振动传递机理，提出结构优化匹配设计与自适应前馈主动隔振控制策略。
