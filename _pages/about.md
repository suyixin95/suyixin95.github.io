Hi, I am Yixin SU (苏一新), who received my Ph.D. in Dynamics and Control from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/) in 2021, under the supervision of Prof. Suyuan Yu and Prof. Gexue Ren. I was also jointly trained at the [University of Bath](https://www.bath.ac.uk/) (UK) with Prof. Patrick S. Keogh, Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering. My doctoral research centered on multibody dynamics theory, intelligent control, nonlinear vibration, vibration suppression, and dynamic design methods for magnetic suspension systems.

大家好，我是苏一新。我于 2021 年在[清华大学](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/)获得动力学与控制专业博士学位，师从于溯源教授与任革学教授，并曾在英国[巴斯大学](https://www.bath.ac.uk/)与英国皇家工程院院士 Patrick S. Keogh 教授联合培养。我的博士研究聚焦于多体动力学理论、智能控制、非线性振动、振动抑制以及磁悬浮系统动力学设计方法。
